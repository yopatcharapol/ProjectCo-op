## 🏛️ 2. เอกสารอ้างอิงสถาปัตยกรรมทั้ง 4 กลุ่มในวงการวิชาการ

### กลุ่มที่ 1: Tree-based & Synchronized TDMA
* **LoraBlink (Slotted Collision-Free Protocol):**
  * M. Bor, J. E. Vidler, and U. Roedig, "LoRa for the Internet of Things," *Proceedings of the 2016 International Conference on Embedded Wireless Systems and Networks (EWSN)*, 2016.
  * ลิงก์: [https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/2893711.2893774](https://dl.acm.org/doi/10.5555/2893711.2893774)
* **MLoRa (Spanning Tree Routing):**
  * S. Feng, J. Chen, and Z. Zhao, "Cost effective routing in large-scale multi-hop LoRa networks," *IEEE INFOCOM 2022 Workshops*, pp. 1–6, 2022.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOMWKSHPS54753.2022.9798302](https://doi.org/10.1109/INFOCOMWKSHPS54753.2022.9798302)
* **Two-Hop Real-Time LoRa (Two-Hop RT-LoRa):**
  * H. P. Tran, W.-S. Jung, D.-S. Yoo, and H. Oh, "Design and implementation of a multi-hop real-time LoRa protocol for dynamic LoRa networks," *Sensors*, Vol. 22, No. 9, Art. 3518, 2022.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.3390/s22093518](https://doi.org/10.3390/s22093518)

### กลุ่มที่ 2: Concurrent Transmission & Clustering
* **CT-LoRa (Concurrent Transmission on LoRa PHY):**
  * C.-H. Liao, G. Zhu, D. Kuwabara, M. Suzuki, and H. Morikawa, "Multi-hop LoRa networks enabled by concurrent transmission," *IEEE Access*, Vol. 5, pp. 21430–21446, 2017.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2017.2762908](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2017.2762908)
* **Cluster-based Spreading Factor Allocation:**
  * G. Zhu, C.-H. Liao, T. Sakdejayont, I.-W. Lai, Y. Narusue, and H. Morikawa, "Improving the capacity of a mesh LoRa network by spreading-factor-based network clustering," *IEEE Access*, Vol. 7, pp. 21584–21596, 2019.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2900010](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2019.2900010)

### กลุ่มที่ 3: Peer-to-Peer & Gateway-Free Mesh
* **Berto et al. (Gateway-Free P2P Mesh):**
  * R. Berto, P. Napoletano, and M. Savi, "A LoRa-based mesh network for peer-to-peer long-range communication," *Sensors*, Vol. 21, No. 13, Art. 4314, 2021.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.3390/s21134314](https://doi.org/10.3390/s21134314)
* **LoRa Mesh Library (Solé et al.):**
  * J. M. Solé, R. P. Centelles, F. Freitag, and R. Meseguer, "Implementation of a LoRa mesh library," *IEEE Access*, Vol. 10, pp. 113158–113171, 2022.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3217215](https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2022.3217215)
  * ซอร์สโค้ด GitHub: [https://github.com/LoRaMesh/LoRaMeshLibrary](https://github.com/LoRaMesh/LoRaMeshLibrary)

### กลุ่มที่ 4: Linear Multi-Hop (แนวท่อยาวและสายส่งไฟ)
* **Linear Multi-hop ในอุโมงค์น้ำใต้ดิน Siena (Abrardo et al.):**
  * A. Abrardo and A. Pozzebon, "A multi-hop LoRa linear sensor network for the monitoring of underground environments: The case of the medieval aqueducts in Siena, Italy," *Sensors*, Vol. 19, No. 2, Art. 402, 2019.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.3390/s19020402](https://doi.org/10.3390/s19020402)
* **Linear Multi-hop สายส่งไฟฟ้าแรงสูง (Huang et al.):**
  * H.-Y. Huang et al., "A LoRa-based optimal path routing algorithm for smart grid," *Proc. 12th Int. Conf. Sens. Technol. (ICST)*, pp. 71–76, 2018.
  * ลิงก์ DOI: [https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSensT.2018.8603612](https://doi.org/10.1109/ICSensT.2018.8603612)
